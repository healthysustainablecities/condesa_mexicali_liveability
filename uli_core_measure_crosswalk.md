## Where our method differs by design

**Large Public Open Spaces Accessibility — Percentage of total land area within 500 m of LPUGS**

GHSCI reports the share of the population, and of sample points, not of land area.  In Condesa, which is platted but largely unoccupied, those differ substantially and the distinction matters: a land area share would count the empty lots, a population share effectively will not.

**Available natural blue spaces — Available natural blue spaces**

The workbook specifies an MNDWI-derived score banded by Euclidean distance from water (< 200 m, 200-500 m, 500-1000 m, 1000-1500 m). What is delivered here is network distance to reachable water, which is an accessibility measure rather than an availability one and will not reproduce those bands.  Reported alongside, not instead of. 'blue_space' covers rivers, canals, drains, lakes, wetlands, beaches and coast, including canals mapped as ways, which is most of Mexicali's blue space; 'public_open_space_with_water' is the narrower question of a park that contains water.

**Walkability Index — Walkability index**

The definitions differ and should not be silently substituted for one another.  GHSCI sums z-scores of the daily living score, neighbourhood population density and intersection density.  The workbook specifies z-scored net residential density + commercial land use proportion + 2 x connectivity + land use mix.  Both the GHSCI index and its components are delivered in natural units so the workbook's version can be assembled centrally; land use mix and commercial land use proportion come from the municipal land use layer (WP06), not from us.
