# Sociodemographic characteristics

[← Back to contents](../README.md)

> Characteristics of the population, such as age, income, educational attainment and household composition, that determine how quality of life is perceived across different groups.

## Subdomains

- **Population.** Characteristics of people living in an area, such as age, gender, income, educational attainment, occupation and population distribution, which make it possible to identify differences in quality of life between groups.
- **Household.** Characteristics of households, such as their composition, size, income, housing conditions and members’ educational attainment, which influence the perception of quality of life.
- **City.** Sociodemographic characteristics of the population and households living in a city, as well as their spatial distribution and socioeconomic conditions, which influence the perception of quality of life across its different areas.

*This domain has no indicator yet: it is described but not scored, so the index is the composite of six domains (Appendix G).*
